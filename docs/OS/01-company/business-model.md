## 2. Suppliers

Two supply lines:

1. **Authorized distributors (Pakistan)** — branded goods with local
   warranty coverage. Advantage: authenticity claims are verifiable.
2. **Direct China sourcing** — better margins, exclusive items, and the
   foundation for 2027 OEM. Requires QC discipline
   (16-knowledge/suppliers.md) and import documentation (18-legal/).

Rule: every product page states its warranty source honestly
(distributor warranty vs. Royal Hardware warranty). This is a trust
asset, not fine print.

## 6. Marketplace Strategy

Default: **own-store first.** Daraz only if used deliberately — a
limited entry-item catalog as an acquisition channel, pushing buyers to
Royal's own store/WhatsApp for range and trade pricing. Never list the
full premium catalog where price-war dynamics erode positioning.
Decision log: 09-ai/decision-logs/own-store-first.md.

## 7. Wholesale

- Tiered quantity pricing: per piece → per box → per carton
  (tiers defined in 03-products/pricing.md).
- Shopify B2B accounts with negotiated price lists for repeat buyers.
- WhatsApp remains the relationship layer; Shopify becomes the ordering
  + record layer. Goal: every reseller reorder takes 2 minutes and is
  recorded.

## 8. Retail

- Premium presentation: real photography (02-brand/photography.md),
  full specs from metafields, honest warranty info, full Urdu support.
- COD supported at launch; card/bank transfer/wallets as available.
- Nationwide via courier; pickup-at-shop preserved (drives foot traffic
  and trust).

## 9. B2B

- Repeat-order flows: saved lists; one-click reorder ships with the
  2027 app/portal.
- Credit terms only for vetted accounts — policy: 12-sop/ + owner
  approval per account.
- Quotation SOP for contractors and industrial buyers
  (11-templates/proposal-quotation.md).
