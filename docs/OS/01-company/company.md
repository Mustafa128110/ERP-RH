## 2. Vision

To be Pakistan's most trusted name in hardware — the company builders,
contractors, and institutions default to when quality cannot be
compromised, online and offline.

## 3. Mission

We bring 35+ years of Jodia Bazaar expertise to every customer in
Pakistan: genuine products, honest advice, fair premium pricing, and
service that treats a single lock buyer with the same respect as a
government tender.

## 4. Values

1. **Trust is the product.** We sell genuine goods only. No copies, no
   mixed stock, no unverifiable claims. If we wouldn't use it, we don't
   sell it.
2. **Advice before invoice.** We tell customers what they actually need,
   even when it's cheaper than what they asked for.
3. **Premium means accountable.** Higher price = we answer the phone
   after the sale. Warranty claims are handled, not dodged.
4. **Old-school word, new-school systems.** A verbal commitment from
   Royal Hardware is binding; our systems (Shopify, inventory, SOPs)
   exist to keep those commitments at scale.
5. **Document everything.** If a process lives only in someone's head,
   it doesn't exist. This is why ROYAL-OS exists.

## 6. Target Markets

Priority order for 2026:

1. **Retail e-commerce (nationwide)** — homeowners, technicians, small
   workshops buying tools, discs, locks, and safety gear online.
2. **B2B / wholesale** — resellers in other cities, contractors, and
   builders buying in quantity (typical order: 10 dozen shovels, 6,000
   discs); served via WhatsApp + Shopify B2B pricing tiers.
3. **Government & industrial (pipeline-building year)** — registration,
   documentation, first small tenders; scale in 2027+.

## 7. Competitors

| Competitor type | They win on | We win on |
|---|---|---|
| Jodia Bazaar wholesalers | Rock-bottom price | Genuine stock, advice, consistency |
| Daraz sellers | Reach, discounts | Authenticity, warranty, expertise |
| Brand outlets/distributors | Brand authority | Range, one-stop, relationship |
| Emerging hardware e-commerce | Head start online | 36 years of trust, deeper catalog, B2B muscle |

**Strategic stance:** never compete on lowest price. Compete on
*certainty* — genuine product, correct advice, reliable delivery, real
warranty. Premium-trusted, priced fairly above the bazaar floor.
