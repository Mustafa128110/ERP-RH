# Royal Hardware — Roadmap

## 2026 — Digital Foundation

### Q1–Q2: Build
- Brand identity from zero (02-brand/): logo, colors, typography.
- Product data foundation: catalog, taxonomy, product template
  (03-products/); photography of top [TBD] SKUs (heroes first).
- Shopify theme build per 06-shopify/ standards (EN + UR), Dawn fork.
- Inventory rules defined and stock counted (03-products/inventory.md).

### Q2–Q3: Launch
- Shopify live with all launch categories complete per expansion rule.
- Courier integration — nationwide + COD; test parcels shipped.
- Analytics baseline: GA4 + Shopify + Search Console (15-analytics/).
- WhatsApp Business formalized: catalog synced, order SOP, tagging.

### Q3–Q4: Grow
- SEO engine running: keyword map, first 12 blog posts (05-marketing/).
- Paid ads live: Google Shopping → Search → Meta retargeting.
- Email + WhatsApp marketing flows live.
- Government supplier registration filed.
- International shipping: evaluate and pilot (limited SKUs, GCC-first);
  go/no-go logged in 09-ai/decision-logs/.

## 2027 — Scale Systems
- **Warehouse:** dedicated space, racking, bin locations, barcode SOPs.
- **ERP:** inventory + accounting + purchasing unified
  (evaluate Odoo vs. local ERP — criteria in 20-future/erp-crm.md).
- **POS:** Shopify POS at the shop counter, unifying walk-in inventory.
- **Mobile app / B2B portal:** B2B reorder-first (priority between app
  and portal reviewed against 2026 data — see 20-future/b2b-portal.md).
- **OEM products:** first Royal-branded line via China supply chain;
  compliance + QC documented.
