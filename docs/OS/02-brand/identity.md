# Brand Book, Part II: Visual Identity System

## 1. Design Concept: "The Standard"

The identity behaves like a hallmark stamped into metal: few
elements, exact proportions, permanent feel. White space is the
luxury; navy is the authority; brass is the seal. If a layout feels
busy, it's off-brand — remove until it feels machined.

## 2. Logo System (execution brief for the designer)

### 2.1 Components
1. **Wordmark** — "ROYAL HARDWARE" in Fraunces (or a hand-refined
   derivative), navy #10263F. "ROYAL" and "HARDWARE" equal visual
   weight; tracking opened on "HARDWARE" (+60 to +100) for a
   plate-engraved feel. Optional brass hairline rule carrying
   "SINCE 1990" in Inter caps, tracked +200.
2. **Monogram** — interlocked R+H drawn on a hexagon geometry
   (the nut/bolt silhouette abstracted — structural, not literal).
   Must survive at 16px favicon and embossed on packing tape.
   Single-color always.
3. **The Seal** (secondary mark) — monogram inside a thin double
   ring with "ROYAL HARDWARE · KARACHI · 1990" circumscribed.
   Usage: packaging, invoice stamp, social watermark, parcel insert.
   The seal is the "genuine" signal made visual.

### 2.2 Lockups
- A. Horizontal: monogram left, wordmark right (primary — web header)
- B. Stacked: monogram above wordmark + tagline (square uses, print)
- C. Wordmark alone (wide, quiet contexts)
- D. Monogram alone (≤ small sizes, avatars, favicon)
- E. Seal alone (packaging, stamps)

### 2.3 Construction & Clear Space
- Base unit **X** = cap-height of "R". Clear space = 1X all sides
  (1.5X for the seal). Monogram hexagon = 3X tall.
- Minimum sizes: wordmark 120px / 28mm · horizontal lockup 140px ·
  monogram 16px / 8mm · seal 48px / 15mm.
- Deliverables required from designer: vector masters (AI/SVG),
  all lockups × all color variants, favicon set, social kit,
  print-ready seal, and a one-page usage sheet. Grid construction
  shown, not just outlines.

### 2.4 Color Variants (complete set — nothing else exists)
1. Navy on white (primary)
2. White on navy (reverse; brass "SINCE 1990" permitted)
3. Brass on navy (ceremonial: seal, packaging accents)
4. Single-black / single-white (fax-grade fallback, forms, embossing)
Never: brass logo on white (contrast), any gradient, any shadow.

### 2.5 Misuse (never)
Stretch · recolor outside variants · effects (shadow/glow/3D) ·
rotate · busy-background placement without a white/navy holding
shape · redraw · crowd the clear space · pair with third-party
logos without a divider rule and equal sizing.

## 3. Layout Principles (how everything is composed)
- **The 70/20/10 rule:** ~70% white/ivory, ~20% navy/ink/steel,
  ~10% brass + semantic. Brass is a seal, not a paint bucket.
- **One brass moment per view.** A rule line, a badge, OR a seal —
  never several competing.
- **Machined alignment:** everything on the 4px grid; hairline
  rules (1px sand, or 1px brass for the one brass moment) do the
  separating; boxes and heavy borders are a last resort.
- **Photography leads, graphics serve.** Layouts are built around
  the product photo or the heritage photo; decoration never
  competes with the goods.

## 4. Applications (specs)

- **Signage (shop):** navy panel, wordmark white, brass rule +
  "SINCE 1990". Night: warm halo lighting, never colored neon.
- **Visiting card:** ivory stock, navy text, brass seal blind-
  embossed or foil (the one place foil is allowed). Urdu side
  mirrored RTL, Nastaliq name.
- **Letterhead/quotation:** white, wordmark top-left, brass
  hairline under header, seal watermark 6% opacity bottom-right.
  This document faces tender officers — it IS the brand in B2B.
- **Packing tape:** navy tape, white monogram repeat — turns every
  carton photo (dispatch-proof content) into brand media.
- **Parcel insert (A6):** front — seal, "Genuine. Since 1990.",
  WhatsApp number large; back — warranty line + reorder nudge, EN/UR.
- **Vehicle/delivery (future):** navy base, white wordmark, brass
  rule. Documented now so it's consistent later.
- **Social kit:** avatar = monogram navy-on-white; cover = navy band,
  wordmark + trust line; post frames = white with 1px sand border,
  seal watermark corner at 8%.
- **Uniform/apron (shop):** navy, brass-thread seal on chest —
  bazaar theater that photographs beautifully for heritage content.
