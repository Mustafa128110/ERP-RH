## 1. Brand Foundation

### 1.1 The Brand Idea
**36 years of Jodia Bazaar trust, delivered anywhere in Pakistan.**

We are not a startup pretending to be established. We are an
established house finally going online. Every brand decision must
feel like it has decades behind it — because it does.

### 1.2 Positioning Statement (internal formula)
For buyers who cannot afford to be wrong about hardware —
contractors, resellers, institutions, and careful households —
Royal Hardware is the trusted supplier that guarantees genuine
goods, correct advice, and reliable delivery, because we have
staked the same counter in Jodia Bazaar on it since 1990.

### 1.3 The Onliness Statement
Royal Hardware is the ONLY hardware supplier that combines
36 years of wholesale-market standing with a modern, bilingual,
nationwide commerce experience — the bazaar's word, at internet
scale.

### 1.4 Brand Archetype
Primary: **The Sage** (knows the trade, gives true counsel).
Secondary: **The Ruler** (order, standards, permanence — "Royal").
Never: The Jester (gimmicks), The Outlaw (discount rebellion).
Practical meaning: we teach and we assure. We never shout, tease,
or beg.

### 1.5 Brand Pillars (everything ladders to these three)
1. **GENUINE** — verifiable authenticity: honest warranty sources,
   batch inspection, real photography.
2. **KNOWING** — 36 years of counsel: advice before invoice, buying
   guides, correct answers.
3. **RELIABLE** — kept promises: honest stock, on-time dispatch,
   replacement-first service.

The brand promise in the market's own words:
**"Asli maal, sahi mashwara, waqt par."**
(Genuine goods, right advice, on time.)
Every claim we ever make must map to one pillar. If it doesn't, cut it.

## 2. Personality

If Royal Hardware were a person: the senior shopkeeper every trader
in the bazaar sends customers to — calm, precise, generous with
advice, never overselling, never flustered. A clean pressed shirt
in a dusty market. Steel in the handshake, warmth in the tea.

| Trait | Means | Never becomes |
|---|---|---|
| Assured | States facts plainly, no hedging | Arrogant, dismissive |
| Straightforward | Plain words, real numbers | Blunt, cold |
| Respectful | PKR 500 buyer = PKR 5 lakh tender | Servile, flattering |
| Precise | Exact sizes, weights, dates | Pedantic, jargon-heavy |
| Warm, not loud | Hospitality, patience | Hype, exclamation marks |

## 3. Verbal Identity

### 3.1 Voice Definition
We write the way a master tradesman speaks to a valued customer:
short sentences, concrete nouns, numbers where numbers belong,
and one moment of genuine warmth. English lean, Urdu dignified.

### 3.2 Register by Context

| Context | Register | Sample |
|---|---|---|
| Product pages | Precise, confident | "Hardened steel shackle, 10mm. Rated for outdoor use." |
| Marketing | Warm authority | "The disc Karachi workshops reorder by the box." |
| Guides/blog | Teaching, generous | "Match the bore first; everything else follows." |
| Support | Patient, solution-first | "Send a photo of the part — we'll match it." |
| B2B/tender | Formal, exact | "Quotation valid 15 days. Delivery within 7 working days." |
| Problems | Own it, fix it | "We sent the wrong size. Replacement ships today, courier on us." |

### 3.3 Vocabulary

Words we own (use freely): genuine · since 1990 · counter · batch ·
carton · trade rate · site-ready · inspected · held (prices) · word.

Words we never use: cheapest · unbeatable · crazy/mega/blowout ·
hurry · limited time (unless literally true) · 100% (as hype) ·
"best quality" (show the spec instead).

Punctuation law: one exclamation mark per YEAR of marketing, maybe.
No ALL CAPS. No emoji floods (one functional emoji max in WhatsApp,
zero in web copy).

### 3.4 Urdu Voice
- Register: respectful آپ, never تم. The tone of a خاندانی دکان —
  an establishment, not a stall.
- Trade terms stay in English where the trade speaks English:
  "grinder", "disc", "drill machine", "COD". Forced translations
  read as unfamiliarity, not respect.
- Urdu is written AS Urdu — rhythm and idiom — never transliterated
  English sentence structure. Machine draft → human ear, always.
- Numerals: Western digits in prices/specs on both locales (trade
  convention).

### 3.5 Message Architecture (what we say first, second, third)
1. Lead: the buyer's job/problem (never our name first).
2. Prove: the spec, the pillar (genuine/knowing/reliable).
3. Invite: one action (buy, WhatsApp, read the guide).
This order is the house style for every page, ad, post, broadcast.

### 3.6 Tagline System
- Permanent lockup line: EN **Trusted since 1990.** ·
  UR **بھروسے کا نام، 1990 سے**
- Campaign line (launch year): "Jodia Bazaar quality, delivered
  nationwide."
- Reserve: "Genuine. Guaranteed. Since 1990."
Rules: the lockup line lives with the logo; campaign lines live in
headlines; the two never appear stacked in the same block.

## 4. Brand Behavior (what we never do)
- Compete on "cheapest" language — it liquidates 36 years of equity.
- Imply anything non-genuine, or overstate a security/safety rating.
- Fake scarcity, fake discounts from inflated anchors, countdown
  theater.
- Foreign stock photos presented as ours; AI images presented as
  photos.
- Public arguments. (Resolve in DM; reply publicly with the
  resolution.)
- Copy a competitor's design or words. We're the one being copied.
