# Brand Book, Part IV: Photography Art Direction

Photography carries the GENUINE pillar. It is the largest single
lever of premium perception on the site — budget time like it.

## 1. The Three Registers

### A. Catalog (white — the standard)
Pure #FFFFFF sweep, soft twin-source light (big diffusers), shadow
almost dissolved (5–8% soft contact shadow only). Square 2000px+
master, product ~80% of frame. Per product: front · ¾ · working-part
detail · scale reference. True color is a contract: the buyer must
receive exactly what the photo promised.

### B. Proof (macro — the genuineness evidence)
Macro texture: abrasive grain, mesh layers, weld beads, engraved
markings, handle grain. Raking light to raise texture. One proof
shot minimum per hero product. These images do what the counter
handshake used to do.

### C. Heritage & Work (the story)
The shop, the lanes, the counter, cartons on hand-trucks, working
hands. Natural light, warm but honest grade (slight warmth allowed
HERE only). People: dignity framing — craftsmen at work, never
posed grins with thumbs-up. Kodak-moment honesty, not stock-photo
theater. Shot once per season; used everywhere.

Plus the **Dispatch stream**: packed cartons leaving, tape visible
(navy monogram tape = free branding in every frame). Phone-shot is
fine; honest scale captions ("6,000 discs to Multan").

## 2. Grading & Treatment
Catalog/Proof: neutral, true-to-life, no filters. Heritage: gentle
warm lift, blacks kept honest, no HDR crunch, no orange-teal.
NEVER: heavy vignettes, fake bokeh, AI-generated "photos", supplier
renders passed as photography (temporary placeholders allowed for
non-hero SKUs only, flagged for replacement).

## 3. Practical Kit (owner-achievable)
White sweep + two diffused lights (or bright window + reflector) ·
any 20MP+ camera or flagship phone · tripod · gray card for color.
Consistency beats equipment: same setup, same angles, every session
(12-sop/photography.md).
