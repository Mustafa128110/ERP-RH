# Ideas — The Inbox With a Pipeline

Nothing dies in a WhatsApp self-note again. But an inbox without
a pipeline is a landfill — so:

## The pipeline
CAPTURED (one line, anyone, any time) → SHAPED (owner adds: what
it is · which pillar/persona it serves · rough cost) → routed:
- serious + strategic → 20-future/ (gets the full format there)
- serious + operational → an SOP/doc patch or experiment (EXP-NNN)
- not now → PARKED with a revisit date
- not us → DECLINED with one line why (see best-practices
  rejection ledger for positioning conflicts)

## Entry format
- [ ] [YYYY-MM-DD] [who] — [the idea, one line]
      → [SHAPED note when added] → [route + date]

## Review: monthly, 10 minutes, alongside the question-log review
(the two feed each other — customer questions are idea ore).
Parked items resurface automatically at their revisit date; a
parked idea passed over twice is either promoted or declined —
no third parking.
