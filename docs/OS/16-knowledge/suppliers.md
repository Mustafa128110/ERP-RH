# Suppliers — The Crown Jewels

The single most valuable file in ROYAL-OS: the supplier judgment
that took 36 years to earn, in a format the business owns.
Consumers: WF-3 reorder decisions · SOP 02 receiving · the 2027
OEM program (20-future) · any succession scenario.
Access: owner + named staff only. Commercial terms stay here,
never in public-facing docs.

## Entry format — one section per supplier

### [Supplier name] — [what we buy from them]
**Profile:** location · years trading with us · contact person +
channel · backup contact.
**Reliability grade:** [A/B/C — owner's call, revised on
evidence] with the one-line why.
**Lead times:** promised vs ACTUAL (rolling record — SOP 02
captures every delivery):
| Order date | Promised | Actual | Notes |
**QC record:** per-shipment sample results (SOP 02 step 3):
| Shipment | Sample | Failures | Issue | Resolution |
Consistency note: does box 480 match box 1? (the reseller
promise depends on this line)
**Price history:** date · item · rate · payment terms · what
moved it (currency? volume? negotiation?).
**Negotiation notes:** what works with this supplier, what
doesn't; concessions won and given; relationship debts in either
direction.
**Terms:** MOQ · payment (advance %/LC/credit) · who pays freight
· defect-claim handling (and whether they honor it in practice —
the practice line matters more than the agreement line).
**Backup:** who else can supply this line, at what penalty in
price/quality/time.
**China-specific fields:** shipping route + typical door-to-door
days · customs/clearing notes per shipment · reference-unit
retained? (SOP 02) · OEM potential: would we put our name on
their product? [yes/maybe/never + why].

## Standing analyses (owner, quarterly)
- Concentration risk: any category >70% from one supplier gets a
  developed backup, deliberately.
- Lead-time truth table: the promised-vs-actual gaps ARE the
  safety-buffer inputs (inventory.md) — update buffers from this
  file, not from optimism.
- OEM shortlist: the "would we put our name on it" column,
  reviewed against volume data when 20-future/oem-products.md
  activates.
